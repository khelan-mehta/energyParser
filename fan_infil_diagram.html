Reading Fan Power & Infiltration from the TRACE PDF

A plain-English look at the three numbers the parser builds — and how modelers get them by hand.
TOTAL FAN POWER
1 · In the PDF
The "Fans" table lists every fan.
Each row shows Flow Rate (cfm) and Power (kW).
154 cfm  ·  0.03 kW
→
2 · What the parser does
Add up the kW of every fan row.
Σ (all fan kW) = Total Fan Power
Baseline = 44 supply + exhaust fans.
→
3 · Result
1.71 kW
(proposed ≈ 0.5 kW)
FAN POWER / CFM
1 · Inputs
Total fan power + total airflow.
1.71 kW = 1,710 W
14,689 cfm total
→
2 · The math
Watts ÷ airflow.
1,710 W ÷ 14,689 cfm
Convert kW→W first, so the answer lands in W/CFM.
→
3 · Result
0.12 W/CFM
NOT kW/CFM — that'd be 1000× off.
INFILTRATION RATE
1 · In the PDF
Each zone lists "Infiltration cfm".
It's printed twice per zone — once in the cooling airflow box, once in heating.
→
2 · What the parser does
Sum all zones, halve it, divide by wall area.
(Σ infil cfm ÷ 2) ÷ facade ft²
÷2 undoes the cooling+heating double-print.
→
3 · Result
CFM/ft² facade
Airflow per ft² of exterior wall.

How energy modelers usually get these by hand

Fan power

Pull each fan's kW from the equipment/fan schedule (or the sim's SV-A / fan report), sum them, then divide watts by design supply CFM. Typical fan power intensity is 0.3–1.0 W/CFM — always watts per cfm, never kW/cfm.

Infiltration

Set a design leakage per code — e.g. 0.10–0.40 CFM/ft² of exterior wall at a reference pressure (ASHRAE 90.1 App G / Title 24), or convert a blower-door ACH50 to cfm. The rate is applied per façade area, exactly what the parser back-calculates.
